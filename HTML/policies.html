Hotel Policies

Terms, Conditions, and Important Information

Check-in and Check-out Policies

Standard Check-in Time

Check-in begins at 3:00 PM and latest check-in time is 11:00 PM. If arriving later, please inform us in advance.

Standard Check-out Time

Check-out is at 11:00 AM on your departure date. Late check-out may be available for an additional fee, subject to availability.

Early Check-in

Early check-in is available from 12:00 PM, depending on room availability and at no additional cost.

Late Check-out

Late check-out requests must be made at the front desk. A 50% room charge will apply for check-out after 2:00 PM.

Cancellation Policy

Free Cancellation

Reservations can be cancelled free of charge up to 7 days before the date of arrival.

Late Cancellation

Cancellations made within 7 days of arrival will incur a charge equivalent to the first night's stay.

No-Show Policy

No-shows will be charged for the entire stay as per the booking terms. Guests are advised to cancel properly if unable to arrive.

Group Bookings

For group bookings (10+ rooms), a 10% non-refundable deposit is required, with final cancellation 14 days prior to arrival.

Payment Policy

Accepted Payment Methods

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, digital wallets, bank transfers, and cash payments at the front desk.

Deposit Requirements

A valid credit card is required to secure your reservation. Full payment is due at check-in unless other arrangements have been made.

Currency

Room rates are displayed in USD. Other currencies may be accepted at current exchange rates, subject to 3% administrative fee.

Invoices and Receipts

Itemized invoices and receipts are available upon request. Tax and service charges will be added as per local regulations.

Guest Code of Conduct

Noise Policy

Guests are requested to keep noise levels low, especially between 10:00 PM and 8:00 AM. Quiet hours are strictly enforced.

Smoking Policy

Smoking is strictly prohibited in all indoor areas except designated smoking zones. Violators will be charged a cleaning fee of $300.

Pet Policy

Pets are welcome with prior arrangement. A fee of $50 per night applies. Pets must be crated when left unattended in rooms.

Guest Behavior

All guests must comply with hotel rules. Management reserves the right to request guests to vacate if they violate policies or disturb others.

Damage and Loss Policy

Room Damage

Guests are responsible for any damage caused to rooms, furniture, or equipment during their stay. Costs will be charged to the guest's account.

Lost and Found

Items left behind will be kept for 30 days. After this period, unclaimed items will be donated or discarded. Contact us immediately if items are lost.

Safe Deposit

We provide complimentary safe deposit boxes at the front desk for valuables. The hotel is not liable for items left in rooms.

Liability

The hotel is not responsible for loss, theft, or damage to personal belongings unless stored in the safe deposit box.

Accessibility and Special Services

Accessible Rooms

We offer fully accessible rooms with wheelchair access, grab bars, and accessible bathrooms. Request these rooms when making your reservation.

Service Animals

Service animals are welcome free of charge. Emotional support animals may be subject to pet policies and fees.

Special Requests

We are happy to accommodate special requests such as crib rentals, extra beds, or room preferences at no additional cost if available.

24/7 Assistance

Our concierge team is available round-the-clock to assist with any special needs or requests.

Privacy and Data Protection

Guest Information

We collect guest information only for booking, billing, and service improvement purposes. Your data is protected with industry-standard security.

Communication

We may contact you via email or phone for promotional offers. You can opt-out of marketing communications at any time.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not share personal information with third parties without your explicit consent, except for payment processing and necessary hotel operations.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies to enhance your experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings if preferred.

Questions About Our Policies?

Contact our guest services team for more information or clarification on any policy.
